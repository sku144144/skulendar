Privacy Policy

Skulendar · updated 21 September 2026 · Русский

In short. The app collects nothing about you and has no server of its own. Your notes stay on your device. If you switch backup on, copies go to your own Google Drive — and nowhere else.

What is stored on your device

Notes, attachments (photos, video, voice recordings, drawings), reminders and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your device. The developer has no access to them.

Device permissions

  • Microphone — requested only when you tap the record button to add a voice note. The recording is saved as an attachment on your device and is not sent anywhere (except into your own Google Drive, if you enable backup).
  • Notifications, alarms and reminders — to show your reminders at the exact time you set, including on the lock screen, and to restore them after a reboot.
  • Internet — only for Google Drive backup and for public holidays (see below).

Public holidays

If you choose a country for holidays in the settings, the app downloads the list of public holidays for that country from the free public service date.nager.at. The request contains only the year and the country code; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to that service. No notes or other personal data are sent. With no country selected, no such request is made.

Google Drive backup

This feature is off by default and only runs once you enable it. With your consent, the app:

  • creates a folder named Skulendar in your Google Drive;
  • uploads an archive of your notes along with your attachment files;
  • reads the contents of that folder, so it can skip files already uploaded and restore your data when you ask it to.

The files are yours and they sit in your account. The developer never sees or receives them. There is no server in between: the app talks to Google Drive directly from your device.

Which Google permission is requested

One, and only one: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This grants access solely to files the app itself created. The rest of your Drive is invisible to it — as a technical fact, not as a promise.

The app does not request access to your whole Drive, nor to Gmail, contacts or Google Calendar.

Revoking access

At any time, revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Backups already created stay in your Drive; you can delete them by hand like any other files.

What the app does not do

  • no analytics, no usage tracking;
  • no advertising and no ad trackers;
  • no sharing with third parties — there is nothing to share and nobody to share it with;
  • no accounts, no stored passwords.

Use of Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google Drive is used only to create and restore backups at your request. It is not transferred to third parties, not used for advertising, and not read by humans.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about them — nor, for that matter, about anyone else.

Changes

Should this policy change, the new version will appear on this page with a new date at the top.
