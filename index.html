Skulendar

A calendar with notes, for Android

Skulendar keeps a note for every day, plus standalone notes of their own. It handles attachments, drawing and reminders — and everything lives on your device.

What it does

  • Notes attached to dates, plus standalone notes organised into tabs
  • Rich text: lists, checklists, colours, links
  • Attachments — photos, video, voice recordings, drawings
  • Reminders with a sound of your choosing
  • Public holidays for your country
  • Backups, locally and — if you want it — to Google Drive

Where your data lives

Notes are stored on the device. The app sends them to no server, for the simple reason that it has none.

Google Drive backup is optional and off until you turn it on. Backups go to your own Drive, into a folder named Skulendar. The developer cannot reach them.
